🔐 SshLens

Paste SSH public keys - one line or a whole authorized_keys file. Algorithm, bits, comment, both fingerprints, and the OpenSSH randomart for each digest, all decoded in your browser. Verified byte-for-byte against ssh-keygen. About

Nothing is uploaded. Fingerprints are computed from the decoded blob with pure-JS SHA-256 and MD5; randomart uses the OpenSSH drunken-bishop algorithm. Every corpus key is verified against ssh-keygen -l / -lv for both digests (tests/).
